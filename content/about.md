awswnv.win is a practical help site for Windows laptop owners. We publish step-by-step solutions for the problems people actually run into: batteries that won't charge, blue screens, broken Wi-Fi, slow performance, and Windows updates gone wrong.

## How Our Guides Are Made

Each guide starts from a real problem — usually a specific error code, symptom, or question users search for. We then:

1. **Identify the likely causes**, from the most common to the rarest.
2. **Test and document fixes in order**, starting with the safest and simplest.
3. **Explain the "why"**, not just the clicks, so you understand what you're changing.
4. **Answer follow-up questions** in an FAQ section at the end of every article.

## What We Cover

- **Battery & Power** — charging problems, battery health, calibration, power settings
- **Windows Errors & BSOD** — blue screens, stop codes, boot loops
- **Drivers & Devices** — Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, touchpad, audio, and driver issues
- **Performance & Speed** — high disk/RAM/CPU usage, slow startup
- **Windows Update** — stuck updates, error codes, rollbacks

## A Note on Safety

We always lead with non-destructive fixes and clearly mark steps that carry risk (like registry edits). Our [disclaimer](/disclaimer/) has the full details — please read it before following any guide.
