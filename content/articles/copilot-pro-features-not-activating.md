You paid for Copilot Pro, but Copilot still treats you like a free user: no priority access, no Pro models, no Pro-only features. It is a uniquely frustrating failure because money has changed hands and the product has not kept its side of the bargain. The good news is that activation problems are almost always account or licence-refresh issues, not billing failures — and they are fixable without contacting support in most cases.

The first distinction is between "Pro is not on this account" and "Pro is on the account but this device has not noticed." The first is a subscription problem to check on Microsoft's account pages. The second is a refresh problem to fix by signing out and back in or updating the app. Checking the subscription first saves you from resetting an app that was never the problem.

## Why this happens

- **Wrong Microsoft account signed in:** Pro belongs to the purchasing account, and a different signed-in account shows free features.
- **Subscription still processing:** A new purchase can take time to propagate through Microsoft's systems.
- **Licence not refreshed on device:** The app caches licence status and may not re-check until sign-out or update.
- **Outdated Copilot app:** Older app versions may not recognise or display Pro features correctly.
- **Work vs personal account confusion:** Pro purchased on a personal account does not activate on a work account, and vice versa.
- **Payment or subscription issue:** A failed payment, expired card, or cancelled subscription silently drops Pro status.
- **Regional availability differences:** Some Pro features roll out by region, so a feature may be absent even with active Pro.

## How to fix it

Work through these in order — diagnose first, then act.

### Fix 1: Verify the subscription on the right account

Start at the authoritative source: your Microsoft account's subscriptions page. This tells you definitively whether Pro is active and on which account, ending all guessing.

1. Sign into your Microsoft account on the web.
2. Open the Subscriptions section.
3. Confirm Copilot Pro appears as active.
4. Note exactly which account holds it.
5. Check for payment problems or expiry dates.
6. If Pro is absent or expired, resolve billing first — no app fix can substitute for an inactive subscription.

### Fix 2: Sign into Copilot with the purchasing account

If Pro is active but on a different account than the one signed into Copilot, switching accounts is the entire fix. This is the most common cause and takes under a minute.

1. Open Copilot and check the signed-in account.
2. Compare it with the account holding the Pro subscription.
3. If different, sign out of Copilot.
4. Sign back in with the purchasing account.
5. Wait a few minutes for licence status to refresh.
6. Check for the Pro badge and Pro features.

### Fix 3: Force a licence refresh

Even on the right account, Copilot may cache an old free status. Signing out and back in, or updating the app, forces a fresh licence check that usually activates Pro immediately.

1. Sign out of Copilot completely.
2. Close the app.
3. Reopen it and sign back in with the Pro account.
4. Wait several minutes without closing the app.
5. Check for Pro indicators.
6. If still absent, update Copilot from the Store and repeat.

### Fix 4: Update Copilot and Windows

Older app versions may not display or unlock Pro features even when the licence is valid. Updating both the app and Windows aligns feature availability with your subscription.

1. Open the Microsoft Store and update Copilot.
2. Open Settings, go to Windows Update, and install pending updates.
3. Restart after updating.
4. Sign into Copilot with the Pro account.
5. Check for Pro features.
6. Try Pro features in both the app and the web version to compare.

### Fix 5: Test Pro on the web

The web version of Copilot is a useful control: if Pro works there but not in the app, the app is the problem; if it fails there too, the subscription or account is the problem. This split directs the rest of your troubleshooting.

1. Open Copilot on the web and sign in with the Pro account.
2. Check for Pro indicators and try a Pro feature.
3. If Pro works on web, focus on the app — repair or reset it.
4. If Pro fails on web too, the issue is account or subscription level.
5. Re-verify the subscription and account match.
6. Contact Microsoft support if web Pro also fails despite an active subscription.

### Fix 6: Repair or reset the Copilot app

When the subscription is valid and web Pro works, but the app still shows free features, the app's cached licence state is likely corrupt. Repairing or resetting it forces a clean licence fetch.

1. Open Settings, go to Apps, then Installed apps.
2. Find Copilot and open Advanced options.
3. Choose Repair and test Pro features.
4. If Repair fails, choose Reset.
5. Sign back in with the Pro account.
6. Wait for licence refresh and confirm Pro activates.

### Fix 7: Check for regional or rollout limitations

Some Pro features arrive by region or in stages. If Pro is active and most features work but one specific feature is missing, it may simply not have reached your region yet rather than being broken.

1. Confirm which specific Pro feature is missing.
2. Check Microsoft's Copilot Pro announcements for that feature's availability.
3. Compare with the web version, which often receives features first.
4. If the feature is region-limited, wait for rollout.
5. Keep the app updated so the feature arrives promptly.
6. For billing disputes, use Microsoft's account support rather than app troubleshooting.

## Closing

Copilot Pro activation failures are almost always account or licence-refresh problems: the wrong account signed in, a stale cached status, or an outdated app. Verify the subscription, sign in with the purchasing account, and force a refresh before assuming anything is broken. If the steps above still leave Pro inactive, check the official guidance at [Microsoft Support for Windows](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows) for account and app troubleshooting. Once Pro activates, it should stay active across all your devices under that account — and if it drops again, the same three checks will almost always bring it back.
